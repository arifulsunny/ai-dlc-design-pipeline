## Review Questions

- Does every P0 requirement map to a user task and flow?
- Are role and permission decisions clear?
- Are screen specs buildable?
- Are all relevant states documented?
- Are accessibility risks addressed?
- Are scalability and edge cases covered?
- Does handoff include frontend, backend, component, and acceptance criteria?
- Are design changes versioned?
