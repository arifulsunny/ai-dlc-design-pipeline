# Design Change Log

## Purpose

This file records every meaningful design change after the initial approved design.

## Change Log Format

| Version | Date | Changed Area | Change Type | Requested By | Approved By | Related Issue/PR | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v1.0.0 | 2026-05-21 | Initial pipeline structure | Initial design | Repo owner | Pending | TBD | Draft |

## Quality Gate

- No design change is final until this log is updated.
- Changes that alter behavior, layout, accessibility, handoff, or design system rules need a corresponding design decision record when the reason matters.
